01 / The problem

You are losing money because you are doing $15/hour admin work instead of $150/hour trade work.

Nobody started a trade business to answer texts at nine at night. But the phone, the quote book, and the follow-up list don’t care that you’re on a ladder. Every hour spent on admin is an hour not spent on billable work—and the leak never shows up as a line item. It shows up as a thinner week.

Leak 01 / The missed call

A homeowner calls while you’re on a ladder. It rings out. By four o’clock they’ve hired the next name on the search results. No record, no callback, no job.

Leak 02 / The quiet quote

The quote was right. It just went out and then sat there. Day four. Day nine. The customer assumes you lost interest, because you never said otherwise. The quote dies in silence.

Leak 03 / The invisible business

The work was good. Nobody asked for a review. The next customer reads the three reviews you have and calls someone who has sixty. Retention never gets requested.

This isn’t a marketing problem; it’s an infrastructure problem. Marketing gets attention. Infrastructure keeps the work.

07 / Questions

Straight answers.

If yours isn’t here, the audit is the fastest place to get it answered with your numbers on the table.

Is this just a chatbot?

No. A chatbot answers questions. This is an operational system: it captures the lead, books the slot, follows the quote, requests the review, and writes the record to your CRM. The conversation is one small part of the machinery.

Do I have to change my CRM?

No. We connect to the CRM you already use—Jobber, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, HubSpot, or a spreadsheet if that is what runs the office. Your records stay in your system, owned by you.

How long does the install take?

Most installs go live in two to three weeks. One week to read the operation, one to build and test against live enquiries, one day to switch the numbers over. The system is tested on real calls before it handles all of them.

We already have someone answering the phone.

Good. The system handles what happens when they are on another line, after hours, or on the weekend. It also removes the chasing and data entry they should not be doing in the first place.

What does the audit cost?

Nothing. The audit is a working session, not a sales call. You leave with the leaks quantified in dollars, whether or not we install anything.

What happens after month one?

The system keeps running. We check it monthly, correct drift, and adjust the rules as your season and crew change. You do not manage it, and you do not call us to keep it working.

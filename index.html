01 / The weight

The work isn’t the weight. The admin is.

You can run a crew. You can read a site. What you can’t do is answer the phone from a crawlspace, send the quote from the truck, chase the follow-up at nine at night, and still be home for dinner.

  1. 2:14 PM / The call

    A homeowner calls while you’re on a ladder. It rings out. By four o’clock they’ve hired the next name on the search results.

  2. Day four / The quote

    The number was right. It just arrived after the other guy’s. Speed reads as competence, even when it shouldn’t.

  3. 9:40 PM / Second shift

    The day is over. You’re in the truck returning calls, confirming tomorrow, and answering a text that came in at noon.

  4. Monday / The backlog

    Payroll cleared. Follow-ups didn’t. The week starts already owing you something.

None of it is hard work. All of it is constant. Constant is what wears an owner down.

02 / The systems

Installed machinery. Not advice.

Seven modules, each built against your pricing, your service area, and your rules. Install one or install the whole floor. None of them need a reminder.

  1. 01

    Intake

    Every enquiry is captured, qualified, and written into the calendar — call, form, text, or web. Nothing sits in an inbox.

  2. 02

    Dispatch

    Jobs are assigned to the right truck with address, scope, and access notes before the day starts. No morning phone tree.

  3. 03

    Follow-up

    Every open quote gets a written follow-up on a schedule until there is a decision. Silent quotes stop going silent.

  4. 04

    Quoting

    Quotes are built from your pricing and sent within the hour. They are tracked until accepted, declined, or expired.

  5. 05

    Review management

    Completed jobs request the review at the right moment. Anything under four stars reaches the office before it goes public.

  6. 06

    Missed-call recovery

    A missed call gets a text back inside sixty seconds, and a booked slot when they reply. The lead stays with you.

  7. 07

    Scheduling

    The calendar fills from your rules: service windows, drive time, crew skill, and weather. No double-booked mornings.

Seven modules. Installed alone or as one system. Each has a defined input, a defined handoff, and one job to do.

04 / The install

Audit. Build. Install. Run.

A short engagement with a long tail. Most of the work happens before the system speaks to a single customer.

  1. 01Week 1

    Audit

    We read the phone log, the inbox, the quote book, and the calendar. We find where work leaks and put an hourly number on it.

  2. 02Weeks 2–4

    Build

    We configure each module around your pricing, service windows, crew skills, and the way you actually talk to customers. Then we test it on live enquiries.

  3. 03One day

    Install

    We connect the number, calendar, CRM, and review flow. Your team gets a one-page runbook. No training course, no new dashboard to babysit.

  4. 04Ongoing

    Run

    The system answers, routes, follows up, and books. We check it monthly and correct drift. You don’t call us to keep it working.

The point

At 2:14 in the morning, nobody at Solfera is awake. The system answers anyway.

That is the difference between a system and a retainer. You’re not buying our hours. You’re buying back yours.
